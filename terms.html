Terms and privacy

Last updated 5 October 2026. Questions: sage.synapse@gmail.com

Passes

  • After signing in, the first round in each lab is free. A pass opens every round in all labs and every Data case.
  • Exam-year pass: S$69, valid until 31 December 2027. 30-day pass: S$15, valid for 30 days from payment.
  • Both are one-off payments by PayNow or card, handled by Stripe. Nothing renews automatically.
  • A pass covers one student and up to three devices. Further devices can be unlocked by emailing us.
  • Students under 18 should check with a parent or guardian before paying.

Refunds

If you are not happy, email us within 7 days of paying for a full refund, no questions asked. After that, we refund only if the site was unavailable for a long time. A refunded pass stops working on its devices within a day.

What we collect and why

  • Sign-in: name, school, level and email address. We use these to know who uses Synapse Econs, to improve it, and to reply to you.
  • Usage: pages opened, rounds finished and scores, linked to a random device id. We use this to see which labs help and what to improve.
  • Payment: the email given at checkout, the pass bought, the amount and date. We use this to unlock the pass and to handle refunds. Card and bank details go to Stripe and are never seen or stored by us.
  • Learning progress stays in your browser and is not sent to us.

The data is kept in a private Google Sheet that only the Synapse team can open. We do not sell it or share it with anyone else, and we do not show advertising.

Students under 18

Most of our users are junior college students, and many are under 18. If you are under 18, please check with a parent or guardian before buying a pass. If a pass was bought without their agreement, email us and we will refund it.

Keeping and deleting data

We keep sign-in and usage data while the student uses Synapse, and payment records for as long as the law requires for accounts. To see, correct or delete your data, email sage.synapse@gmail.com and we will reply within 30 days.
