Data Response Lab

About 16 marks in each case study come from reading the data: calculate a change, compare two trends, then explain what you see with economics. Each set is a mini case with a Singapore extract and a data table (real figures, or marked as illustrative), in the exam's own format.

How this trains the brain

  • Write first, then check. For explain questions, write your answer before the mark points appear, so you retrieve the chain rather than recognise it.
  • Mark yourself honestly. Tick only the points you actually made. Matching your answer against a mark scheme is how you learn what earns marks.
  • Show your working. Calculation feedback always shows the method, since the method earns the marks.
