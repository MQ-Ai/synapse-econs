Concepts Lab

Every case study rewards exact definitions. This lab drills H1 terms across all three syllabus themes, in both directions: name the definition, and name the term from its definition.

How this trains the brain

  • Both directions. Term → definition builds recognition; definition → term builds the recall you need to name the concept in your own answer.
  • Near-miss choices. Wrong options come from the same topic (MPC vs MSC, change in demand vs change in quantity demanded), so you must know the exact wording.
  • Spacing and a confidence check. Missed terms return three cards later. A lucky guess is not counted as mastered.
