The Labs

One paper. Four skills.

JC1 focus: the labs open on Themes 1 and 2 (scarcity, markets and market failure). Macroeconomics (Theme 3) is kept for JC2 and can be switched on in each lab.

H1 is a single 80-mark case-study paper. Each lab trains one of the four assessment objectives across all three syllabus themes, so you can define it, draw it, explain it and judge it.
